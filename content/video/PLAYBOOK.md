# Aqar & Novixa — Video Marketing Playbook

The finished videos in `exports/`, what each one is for, where to post it, the
ready-to-paste captions (AR + EN), voice-over scripts, a 4-week schedule, and a
small paid plan. Built on the existing strategy in `content/strategy/` and
checked line by line against `content/research/FEATURE_REALITY_CLASSIFICATION.md`.

> **Ground rules used in every video**
> - Footage is **real product UI** (seeded demo, Arabic RTL), tagged on screen
>   "لقطات حقيقية من النظام · بيانات تجريبية". No fake customers, numbers or testimonials.
> - Arabic first, Yemeni-friendly conversational hooks; MSA for feature labels.
> - Sound-off first: every line is on screen, so videos work muted (most feed views).
> - Every video ends with the same three exits: **demo URL · WhatsApp · price anchor**.

## 3. Voice-over (optional, recommended for YouTube / Facebook)

The videos are built to work silent. For a narrated version, record a real
Yemeni voice (best: you or a team member, phone + quiet room is enough), or
generate one for free with **edge-tts**, which has Yemeni Arabic voices
(`ar-YE-SalehNeural` male, `ar-YE-MaryamNeural` female):

```bash
pip install edge-tts
edge-tts --voice ar-YE-SalehNeural --rate=+5% -f vo/AQ01-hero.txt --write-media vo/AQ01-hero.mp3 --write-subtitles vo/AQ01-hero.vtt
# mix under the existing music (music ducked to 35%)
ffmpeg -i exports/AQ01-Hero-9x16.mp4 -i vo/AQ01-hero.mp3 -filter_complex \
  "[0:a]volume=0.35[m];[1:a]adelay=500|500[v];[m][v]amix=inputs=2:duration=first[a]" \
  -map 0:v -map "[a]" -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k exports/AQ01-Hero-9x16-VO.mp4
```

Scripts timed to the cuts are in `vo/` (one `.txt` per video, ~2.3 words/second).

## 4. Four-week launch schedule

Realistic cadence for a small team: **4 posts/week + daily Stories/Status**.
Best windows for Yemen & KSA audiences: **8–10 pm** (Sat–Thu), and **after Jumu'ah** on Friday.

| Week | Sat | Mon | Wed | Thu | Daily Stories / WhatsApp Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Launch | NX01 Novixa (pin it) | **AQ01 Hero** (pin) | AQ09 Try it | AQ02 Roles | Covers + "جرّب الديمو" link sticker |
| 2 — Show the product | AQ03 Client journey | AQ06 Insight | AQ01-EN on LinkedIn | AQ04 Maintenance | Screenshots from the demo, poll: "مكتبك يستخدم إكسل؟" |
| 3 — Convert | AQ08 Explainer (YouTube + FB) | AQ05 Offer | AQ07 Marketers | AQ02 Roles (re-cut cover) | Offer + WhatsApp CTA |
| 4 — Repeat winners | Best performer of weeks 1–3 with a **new hook** | AQ06 Insight | AQ03 | AQ05 Offer | FAQ answers from real DMs |

Also: pin AQ01 on Facebook, add AQ08 as the **featured video** on the Facebook
page and YouTube channel, set AQ01-16x9 as the hero video on the landing pages
(`aqar-landingpage.vercel.app`), and keep AQ08 + the demo link as a WhatsApp
Business **quick reply** (`/demo`).

### Where else to post (free, high intent)
- Yemeni real-estate **Facebook groups** (Mukalla/Sana'a/Aden/Hadhramaut buy-sell-rent groups): post AQ03/AQ04 as *useful* content, not spam; reply to every comment.
- **WhatsApp Status + Business catalog**: add "Aqar license" as a catalog item with AQ05 as its video.
- **LinkedIn** (Novixa page + founder profile): AQ01-EN, NX01, a short "why we built Aqar" text post.
- **YouTube Shorts**: same 9:16 files; they feed the Explainer on the channel.
- **TikTok**: upload the 9:16 files, then add a trending sound *at low volume* in-app.

## 5. Small paid plan (when ready)

Start with **Meta click-to-WhatsApp ads** (Facebook + Instagram): in this region
they convert better than lead forms because the conversation continues where
offices already work.

| Step | Creative | Audience | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| Test (7 days) | AQ05 Offer 4:5 vs AQ01 Hero 4:5 | Yemen (Sana'a, Aden, Mukalla, Hadhramaut) + Yemeni expats in KSA/UAE; interests: real estate, property management, business owners; age 25–55 | $5/day each |
| Scale | Winner + a new hook variant | Same + lookalike of people who messaged | $10–15/day |
| Retarget | AQ08 Explainer | Video viewers ≥50% of AQ01/AQ02/AQ03 (last 30 days) | $3–5/day |

Judge on **cost per WhatsApp conversation** and **demo calls booked**, not views.

### Hook variants to A/B test (first 2 seconds)
1. `مكتبك العقاري كم برنامج يستخدم؟` (current)
2. `لو عندك مكتب عقاري… لازم تشوف هذا`
3. `وداعًا لإكسل والدفاتر في مكتبك العقاري`
4. `موقع ونظام عقاري باسم مكتبك — من $500`
5. `كيف تدير 28 عقار و8 أدوار من شاشة واحدة؟` (demo numbers — say "مثال من الديمو" in caption)

To produce a variant, edit the `Hook` line in `studio/src/videos/Aqar.tsx` and re-render (see `README.md`).

## 6. Measure & iterate

Weekly, per video: 3-second hold rate, average watch %, shares/saves, profile
visits, **WhatsApp conversations**, demo logins (server logs), calls booked.
Keep what holds attention past second 3; re-hook what doesn't. Log results in
`content/` so the next batch is driven by data, not guesses.

## 7. Before you publish — checklist
- [ ] Demo site is up and the accounts in the caption work (password `password`).
- [ ] WhatsApp number answers fast (ads die when replies are slow).
- [ ] Custom cover uploaded from `exports/covers/`.
- [ ] Caption has the demo link + WhatsApp; first comment pinned.
- [ ] No claim beyond `FEATURE_REALITY_CLASSIFICATION.md` (e.g. don't promise e-signature, portal sync or payments — they're not active).
